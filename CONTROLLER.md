## Brief
This file describes the process to pair the Nintendo Switch2 3rd party "PRO" Controllers as a bluetooth device.

## Connect to Windows PC

### Method 1: USB cable
Simply connect the controller to a Windows PC using the provided USB cable. LED1 and LED4 will light up.

### Method 2: Bluetooth
If your computer has Bluetooth capability, enable Bluetooth search, then simultaneously press the **[B]** and **[HOME]** buttons on the controller until LED2 and LED3 flash before releasing your fingers. Find the Bluetooth name **"Xbox Wireless Controller"** on your computer and click to pair. Once successful, LED2 and LED3 will stay lit.

### Method 3: USB receiver
If you purchased the version with a receiver, plug the receiver into a USB port on your computer. Then simultaneously press the **[B]** and **[HOME]** buttons on the controller until LED2 and LED3 flash before releasing your fingers. Wait for a successful connection; LED2 and LED3 will stay lit. If you encounter prolonged connection issues, unplug the receiver and repeat the above steps.

> ※ This controller supports X-INPUT mode games on Windows and game platforms such as Steam.

**Note:** This controller only supports Windows 7 and above. The screenshot and motion control functions are not available.

## Special Prompt

1. In connection modes I, II, and III above, you can press the **HOME** button to reconnect to the most recently connected device.
2. On Android, iOS, and Windows PC devices:
   - Long press **[Capture]** + **[L3]** for about 1 second. The controller vibrates once, meaning the left joystick and D-pad (cross key) functions are switched cyclically.
   - Long press **[Capture]** + **[R3]** for about 1 second. The controller vibrates once, meaning the A/B and X/Y functions are switched cyclically.
